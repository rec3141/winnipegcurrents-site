About this calendar

Six vibe streams named for the waters that shape Manitoba — the Confluence (The Forks, where the Red & Assiniboine meet), the Rapids, the Roar of the Red on a game night, the Deep Waters of the arts, the Open Sky over the lake country, and the Little Ripples for the youngest paddlers — plus On the Horizon for what's coming downstream. Events are gathered each week by a fan-out of agents digging into official calendars, venue listings, breweries, galleries, parks, community boards and box offices, then sorted by feel rather than by date.

Always confirm dates, times and ticket availability on the official event page before heading out — listings shift, and the far-ahead ones especially.
